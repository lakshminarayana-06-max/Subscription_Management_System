Brightloop Subscription Management System

The complete digital backbone for recurring revenue businesses. Eliminate spreadsheets, automate billing cycles, prevent revenue churn, and manage customers, quotations, invoices and payments in one unified platform.

Key Capabilities

  • Subscription Lifecycle: Draft → Quotation → Confirmed → Active → Closed (with Pause/Resume & Renewal).
  • Automated Recurring Invoicing: Scheduled cron jobs generate invoices, apply taxes and discounts, and prevent duplicate billing.
  • Multi-Frequency Plans: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, and Yearly with minimum quantity enforcement.
  • Product Variants: Attribute-based price adjustments (e.g. Odoo brand edition adds ₹560).
  • Flexible Payment Tracking: Support for partial payments, multiple installments, and UPI/Bank/Card methods.
  • Role-Based Security: Strict Administrator, Internal Staff, and isolated Customer Portal boundaries.
  • Executive Dashboard: Real-time calculations of MRR, Revenue Collected, Outstanding Invoices, and Churn.
